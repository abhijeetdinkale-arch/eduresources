\section*{Quick Technical Cheat Sheet \& Tag Index}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Quick Technical Cheat Sheet}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l X}
\toprule
\textbf{HTML Element / Concept} & \textbf{Semantic Meaning \& Standard Usage} \\
\midrule
\texttt{<!DOCTYPE html>} & Triggers HTML5 Standards rendering mode in web browser. \\
\texttt{<meta charset="UTF-8">} & Universal character encoding (supports currency symbols, unicode, emojis). \\
\texttt{<meta name="viewport">} & Mobile responsiveness (\texttt{width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0}). \\
\texttt{<main>} & Dominant document content (strictly one per valid HTML5 page). \\
\texttt{<article>} vs \texttt{<section>} & \texttt{<article>} = self-contained syndication; \texttt{<section>} = thematic grouping with heading. \\
\texttt{<strong>} vs \texttt{<b>} & \texttt{<strong>} = semantic importance/stress; \texttt{<b>} = stylistic bold font. \\
\texttt{<em>} vs \texttt{<i>} & \texttt{<em>} = semantic emphasis; \texttt{<i>} = stylistic italic. \\
\texttt{<dfn>} vs \texttt{<cite>} & \texttt{<dfn>} = definition of term; \texttt{<cite>} = title of creative work. \\
\texttt{colspan} / \texttt{rowspan} & Table attributes to merge cells horizontally / vertically. \\
\texttt{enctype="multipart/..."} & Mandatory \texttt{<form>} attribute for uploading binary files. \\
\texttt{box-sizing: border-box} & Width includes content + padding + border (prevents container blowout). \\
\texttt{var} vs \texttt{let} vs \texttt{const} & Function-scoped vs Block-scoped; \texttt{let}/\texttt{const} have Temporal Dead Zone (TDZ). \\
\texttt{===} vs \texttt{==} & Strict equality (type + value) vs Loose equality (type coercion). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
