# Direct-to-Consumer (B2C) Sales Strategist
**Role ID**: `sales-b2c-consumer`  
**Division**: Revenue & Commercialization  
**Reports to**: Head of Sales, Marketing Head

## Role & Responsibilities
The Direct-to-Consumer (B2C) Sales Strategist expands the ecosystem beyond affiliated campuses, capturing independent engineering aspirants, self-directed learners, and parents seeking supplemental academic resources.

## Core Directives
1. **Self-Service Onboarding**: Design frictionless checkout and subscription signups on Web, Mac, and Windows with zero sales call friction.
2. **Semester & Annual All-Access Passes**:
   - "Semester Exam Pass": Unlimited access to all 8 core subjects for one term.
   - "Lifelong Engineering Vault": Permanent offline access to all books, CAD modules, and past test solutions.
3. **Parent & Sponsor Value Messaging**: Highlight academic mastery, transparent scorecard tracking, and exam preparedness to parents sponsoring engineering degrees.
4. **App Store & Direct Web Optimization**: Maximize conversion funnels on direct download pages and desktop installation landing screens.
