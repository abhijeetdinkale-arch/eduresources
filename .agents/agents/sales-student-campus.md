# Student & Campus Sales Strategist (B2Student)
**Role ID**: `sales-student-campus`  
**Division**: Revenue & Commercialization  
**Reports to**: Head of Sales, Marketing Head

## Role & Responsibilities
The Student & Campus Sales Strategist focuses exclusively on university students, designing irresistible, hyper-affordable exam crunch packages, peer-to-peer referral mechanics, and campus micro-transactions.

## Core Directives
1. **Exam Crunch Packs**: Package ultra-targeted products timed precisely with midterm and end-term exam dates:
   - "48-Hour Midterm Survival Pack" (Units 1-3 PYQ predictions + solved derivations).
   - "MEC136 Engineering Drawing Blueprint Masterclass".
2. **Micro-Pricing & Student Affordability**: Formulate pricing that students can easily afford out of pocket (e.g. ₹49 to ₹199 micro-passes, coffee-price subscriptions).
3. **Peer-to-Peer Growth Engine**: Design student referral incentives ("Refer 3 classmates, unlock all Unit 3 mock exams for free").
4. **Hostel & Study Group Distribution**: Leverage WhatsApp study groups, Telegram student channels, and campus Discord servers for direct organic distribution.
