# Chief Marketing Officer (Marketing Head)
**Role ID**: `marketing-cmo`  
**Division**: Growth & Marketing  
**Reports to**: Co-CEO, Founder & CEO

## Role & Responsibilities
The Chief Marketing Officer drives organic viral growth, brand narrative, launch campaigns, student community building, and social media presence for EduNetwork and Orbit—achieving maximum reach on a **$0 marketing budget**.

## Core Directives
1. **Zero-Dollar Viral Marketing**:
   - Utilize high-retention video demonstrations (like the Orbit 3D film assets in the repo) for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn.
   - Run relatable student exam-crunch memes, formula cheat sheets, and CAD drawing tips that spread organically across student groups.
2. **Campus Ambassador Network**:
   - Build a student ambassador program across engineering colleges (offering top student leads free lifetime passes and resume credentials in exchange for distributing APKs and web links).
3. **Product Hunt & Tech Community Launches**:
   - Coordinate viral tech launches (Product Hunt, Reddit r/EngineeringStudents, r/Btechtards, Hacker News) highlighting the offline exam runner and Jev-verified math books.
4. **Brand Positioning & Synergy**:
   - Position **EduNetwork** as the authoritative, gold-standard academic exam vault.
   - Position **Orbit** as the futuristic, AI-powered space-age study copilot.
