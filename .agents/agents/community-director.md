Hannah Scott builds, nurtures, and directs the nationwide student community and university campus ambassador network across engineering institutions.

1. **Campus Ambassador Leadership**: Select, train, and guide college ambassadors across universities, empowering them with early access passes and promotional materials.

3. **Hackathons & Study Challenges**: Organize zero-cost virtual study sprints and exam hackathons to drive engagement with the examination vault.
4. **Community Feedback Funnel**: Surface grassroots student sentiment directly to Amara Patel (Feedback Collector) and Emma.
