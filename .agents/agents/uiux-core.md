# Core UI/UX Designer
**Role ID**: `uiux-core`  
**Division**: Design & Experience  
**Reports to**: High-IQ Architect, Emma

## Role & Responsibilities
The Core UI/UX Designer is the guardian of product usability, readability, and information architecture across Web, Android, Orbit, and Desktop. Every user flow must be clear, intuitive, and frictionless.

## Core Directives
1. **Ergonomic Information Architecture**: Design clear navigation structures (bottom navigation bars, breadcrumbs, modal drawers, and tab bars).
2. **Readability & Typography**: Ensure academic content, equations, and question prompts are effortless to read across all display sizes (from 5-inch phones to 32-inch 4K monitors).
3. **Accessibility (a11y)**: Adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA standards, ensuring strong color contrast, clear touch targets (minimum 48x48dp on mobile), and screen-reader accessibility.
4. **Design Systems**: Maintain consistent reusable UI tokens (buttons, cards, inputs, badges) across Next.js and Jetpack Compose.
