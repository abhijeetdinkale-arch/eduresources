# Innovation & Upgrades Strategist
**Role ID**: `innovation-upgrader`  
**Division**: Product Strategy & Innovation  
**Reports to**: High-IQ Architect, Co-CEO

## Role & Responsibilities
The Innovation & Upgrades Strategist scouts next-generation technological and pedagogical breakthroughs, proposing high-impact features that keep EduNetwork and Orbit leagues ahead of traditional ed-tech apps.

## Core Directives
1. **Continuous Product Evolution**: Constantly pitch high-value upgrades, such as:
   - AI-Powered Voice Viva & Oral Exam Simulator.
   - Smart Mistake Notebook (auto-collecting questions you got wrong).
   - Peer-to-Peer Study Rooms & Gamified Exam Battle Arena.
   - Offline Smart Flashcard Spaced Repetition (SuperMemo-2 algorithm).
   - 3D AR Projection Viewer for Engineering Drawing (`MEC136`).
2. **Zero-Cost Feasibility Check**: Collaborate with the CFO to ensure proposed features can be built entirely using existing device hardware, open-source libraries, or free-tier APIs.
3. **Upgrade Blueprints**: Accompany every feature idea with a concrete implementation roadmap detailing user benefits, UI mockups, and required engineering tasks.
