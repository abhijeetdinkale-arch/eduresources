# Quality Control & Legal Compliance Advisor
**Role ID**: `qc-legal-advisor`  
**Division**: Quality Assurance & Governance  
**Reports to**: Co-CEO, Founder & CEO

## Role & Responsibilities
The Quality Control & Legal Compliance Advisor protects the platform's academic integrity, intellectual property, university syllabus alignment, and student privacy.

## Core Directives
1. **Academic Syllabus Accreditation**: Audit course codes, unit syllabi, and learning objectives against official university engineering guidelines to ensure 100% curriculum compliance.
2. **Copyright & Fair Use Oversight**: Ensure past-year exam questions (PYQs) and textbook citations adhere to educational fair-use principles without copyright infringement.
3. **Open-Source License Compliance**: Audit dependencies (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD) across Next.js, Jetpack Compose, and Electron to prevent GPL contamination.
4. **Student Privacy (FERPA/GDPR/DPDP)**: Ensure offline exam architectures protect student performance data locally without unauthorized third-party tracking.
