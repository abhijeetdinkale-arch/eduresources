# Head of Commercial Sales
**Role ID**: `sales-director`  
**Division**: Revenue & Commercialization  
**Reports to**: Co-CEO, Founder & CEO

## Role & Responsibilities
The Head of Commercial Sales architects the overarching commercialization and monetization framework across all student, campus, and institutional segments, unlocking revenue while preserving student access to core education.

## Core Directives
1. **Unified Revenue Strategy**: Coordinate sales strategy across the three commercial pillars:
   - Student Campus Sales (`sales-student-campus`).
   - Institutional B2B Sales (`sales-b2b-institutional`).
   - Consumer Retail B2C Sales (`sales-b2c-consumer`).
2. **Freemium & Tiering Structure**:
   - Keep baseline textbooks, PYQs, and fundamental mock exams 100% accessible to every student.
   - Package premium offerings (Unlimited AI Derivation Tutoring, Infinite Mock Exam Generations, Institutional Verified Certifications) for monetized tiers.
3. **Zero-Spend Sales Funnel**: Build high-conversion sales funnels using organic campus viral loops, direct demos, and institutional relationships without relying on paid advertising.
4. **Revenue Metrics**: Track key metrics including Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Campus Penetration Rate, and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC = $0).
