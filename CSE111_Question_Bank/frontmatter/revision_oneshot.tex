\textbf{Concept / Topic} & \textbf{Core Takeaway for Quick Recall} \\
\midrule
\textbf{Machine Language} & Pure binary ($0,1$). Directly run by CPU. Machine-dependent. \\
\textbf{Assembly Language} & Uses mnemonics (\texttt{MOV}, \texttt{ADD}). Requires an Assembler (2-pass). \\
\textbf{Compiler} & Translates entire code to machine/object code before execution. \\
\textbf{Interpreter} & Translates and executes statement-by-statement at runtime. \\
\textbf{Linker \& Loader} & Linker combines object files \& libraries; Loader loads binary into RAM. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Von Neumann (1945)} & Stored-program principle: instructions and data share same memory. \\
\textbf{Generations 1 to 5} & 1st: Vacuum Tubes $\rightarrow$ 2nd: Transistors $\rightarrow$ 3rd: ICs $\rightarrow$ 4th: Microprocessor $\rightarrow$ 5th: AI. \\
\textbf{ALU vs. CU} & ALU does arithmetic \& logic ($+, -$, AND, OR); CU manages timing and execution. \\
\textbf{Buses} & Address bus = unidirectional; Data bus = bidirectional; Control = timing/commands. \\
\textbf{Embedded Computer} & Dedicated microcontroller performing a fixed control task inside an appliance. \\
\midrule
\textbf{CPU vs. GPU} & CPU has few high-speed cores for logic/OS; GPU has thousands of cores for parallel math. \\
\textbf{PC Register} & Stores memory address of the \textit{next} instruction to fetch. \\
\textbf{IR Register} & Stores the instruction \textit{currently being executed}. \\
\textbf{DRAM vs. SRAM} & DRAM = Main memory (capacitors, needs refresh); SRAM = Cache memory (fast, flip-flops). \\
\textbf{SSD vs. HDD} & SSD = Flash memory (no moving parts, shock-proof); HDD = Spinning magnetic disks. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Radix \& Groupings} & Octal = base 8 (group 3 bits); Hex = base 16 (group 4 bits; A=10, B=11, \dots, F=15). \\
\textbf{Binary Fractions} & $0.1_2 = 0.5$, $0.01_2 = 0.25$, $0.001_2 = 0.125$, $0.11_2 = 0.75$, $0.101_2 = 0.625$. \\
\midrule
\textbf{Git (2005)} & Distributed version control by Linus Torvalds. Local history stored in \texttt{.git}. \\
\textbf{Git Workflow} & Working Dir $\xrightarrow{\texttt{git add}}$ Staging Area $\xrightarrow{\texttt{git commit}}$ Local Repo $\xrightarrow{\texttt{git push}}$ GitHub. \\
\textbf{Git Branches} & Movable pointers to commits; \texttt{git switch -c <name>} creates and switches. \\
\midrule
\textbf{AI Milestone} & Coined at Dartmouth Workshop (1956). Narrow AI = specialized tasks. \\
\textbf{ML vs. DL} & Machine Learning learns from data; Deep Learning uses multi-layer neural networks. \\
\textbf{Hallucination} & Generative AI fluently stating incorrect facts or fake references. \\
\textbf{AI Research Tools} & Perplexity AI = web search with citations; NotebookLM = grounded in user's notes. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
