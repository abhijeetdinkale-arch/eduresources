\section{Practice Question Set (50 MCQs)}

\mcqitem{1}{The formal term "Artificial Intelligence" was first introduced at which historical academic event in 1956?}{The Turing Conference}{The Dartmouth Summer Research Project}{The IEEE World Congress}{The Paris Computing Exposition}

\mcqitem{2}{What is the primary objective of Artificial Intelligence as a discipline of computer science?}{Building computers that do not require electricity}{Creating computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence}{Writing operating systems exclusively in binary}{Replacing all human memory}

\mcqitem{3}{A branch of AI where systems automatically learn patterns from data rather than following exclusively hardcoded rules is:}{Machine Learning (ML)}{Microcoding}{Direct Memory Access}{Hardware Emulation}

\mcqitem{4}{Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that relies primarily on:}{Mechanical relays}{Multi-layered artificial neural networks}{Punched card algorithms}{Purely manual decision trees}

\mcqitem{5}{In machine learning, the process of adjusting internal model parameters using a large dataset is called:}{Inference}{Training}{Tokenization}{Formatting}

\mcqitem{6}{In machine learning, applying an already-trained model to produce predictions or answer a user's prompt is called:}{Training}{Inference}{Compiling}{Preprocessing}

\mcqitem{7}{All existing commercial AI tools (such as Siri, ChatGPT, and autonomous driving aids) are categorized as:}{Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)}{Narrow (Weak) AI}{Super AI}{Self-conscious AI}

\mcqitem{8}{What is the fundamental difference between traditional predictive AI and Generative AI?}{Predictive AI only works offline}{Generative AI creates new content (text, images, audio) rather than merely classifying existing data}{Generative AI does not use algorithms}{Predictive AI is based on vacuum tubes}

\mcqitem{9}{When a generative language model produces confident-sounding, fluent statements that are factually false or invented, this is known as:}{Overclocking}{Hallucination}{Paging}{Thrashing}

\mcqitem{10}{How do Large Language Models (LLMs) fundamentally generate text?}{By looking up fixed answers in an encyclopedia}{By repeatedly predicting the most probable next token/word based on context}{By executing SQL queries}{By performing binary division}

\mcqitem{11}{In natural language processing with AI models, what is a "token"?}{A physical coin placed in a machine}{A piece of text such as a word, sub-word, or punctuation mark}{A secure password}{A network cable}

\mcqitem{12}{Which company developed and released ChatGPT?}{Google}{OpenAI}{Microsoft}{Meta}

\mcqitem{13}{Which conversational AI model and assistant was developed by Google with native multimodal capabilities?}{Gemini}{DALL-E}{Midjourney}{Runway}

\mcqitem{14}{What does "multimodal AI" mean?}{The AI can run on multiple monitors}{The AI can understand and process multiple data types simultaneously (e.g., text, images, audio)}{The AI has multiple power cables}{The AI uses multiple operating systems}

\mcqitem{15}{Which of the following is a prominent text-to-image generation tool?}{DALL-E}{Perplexity AI}{Git Bash}{Linux}

\mcqitem{16}{Which text-to-image generation model is developed by Adobe with a focus on commercially safe design assets?}{Midjourney}{Adobe Firefly}{ChatGPT}{NotebookLM}

\mcqitem{17}{In AI image generation, what is "inpainting"?}{Drawing an image from scratch with a mouse}{Modifying, replacing, or repairing a selected specific region within an existing image}{Printing an image on canvas}{Deleting the entire image}

\mcqitem{18}{In AI image generation, what is "outpainting"?}{Deleting the background of a photograph}{Extending the canvas and content of an image beyond its original boundaries}{Exporting an image to PDF}{Reducing image resolution}

\mcqitem{19}{Which AI tool is widely used to create presenter-style video presentations featuring synthetic talking avatars?}{Runway}{Synthesia}{Perplexity AI}{JenniAI}

\mcqitem{20}{Which AI video generation platform is known for text-to-video generation and cinematic motion control?}{Runway}{NotebookLM}{JenniAI}{Midjourney}

\mcqitem{21}{What is a major technical challenge currently faced in AI video generation?}{Generating text subtitles}{Maintaining temporal consistency (preventing faces and clothing from changing between frames)}{Playing video in colour}{Using headphones}

\mcqitem{22}{Which AI-powered research and search tool provides answers grounded in real-time web search with numbered clickable citations?}{Perplexity AI}{Midjourney}{DALL-E}{Synthesia}

\mcqitem{23}{Google's NotebookLM is unique among research assistants because it:}{Searches the entire dark web}{Grounds its answers strictly in the user's uploaded documents and notes}{Generates 3D video animations}{Writes code without prompts}

\mcqitem{24}{Which AI writing assistant is specifically tailored to assist students and researchers with academic drafting and citations?}{JenniAI}{Adobe Firefly}{Midjourney}{Runway}

\mcqitem{25}{The practice of carefully designing and refining input instructions given to an AI model to obtain optimal responses is:}{Hardware debugging}{Prompt engineering}{Machine compilation}{Token stripping}

\mcqitem{26}{Which of the following is an example of a "poor prompt" for an AI model?}{"Explain the fetch-decode-execute cycle in 100 words for a first-year student."}{"Tell me about computers."}{"List 3 differences between RAM and ROM in a table."}{"Summarize the main points of the attached text into 5 bullets."}

\mcqitem{27}{Which of the following is an effective component to include when writing a high-quality prompt?}{A vague one-word topic}{A clearly defined role, context, specific task, and output constraints}{Random punctuation marks}{Excessive typos}

\mcqitem{28}{In prompt engineering, assigning a persona such as \texttt{"Act as an experienced computing lecturer"} establishes the:}{Task}{Role}{Constraint}{Format}

\mcqitem{29}{In prompt engineering, instructions like \texttt{"Use no more than 200 words and present as a bulleted list"} are called:}{Constraints}{Hallucinations}{Inferences}{Tokens}

\mcqitem{30}{What is the recommended approach if an AI assistant provides a response that is too vague or partially incorrect?}{Give up immediately}{Iteratively refine the prompt by providing specific feedback and clearer constraints}{Turn off the computer}{Report a hardware fault}

\mcqitem{31}{How is AI currently utilized in everyday healthcare?}{By replacing human doctors entirely for surgeries}{By analyzing medical imaging scans to highlight anomalies for clinician review}{By manufacturing medical beds}{By generating pharmaceutical advertisements}

\mcqitem{32}{In electronic commerce and entertainment (such as Netflix or Amazon), AI is most commonly used for:}{Cooling data center servers}{Recommender systems that suggest relevant movies or products based on user history}{Designing shipping boxes}{Regulating power voltage}

\mcqitem{33}{How do financial institutions and banks commonly deploy machine learning models in real time?}{To guess lottery numbers}{To detect anomalous transactions and potential credit card fraud}{To print physical paper banknotes}{To replace automated teller machines}

\mcqitem{34}{In navigation apps like Google Maps, how does AI assist commuters?}{By predicting traffic congestion and optimizing travel routes in real time}{By driving the vehicle remotely}{By laying new physical roads}{By manufacturing GPS satellites}

\mcqitem{35}{In predictive maintenance within manufacturing plants, AI models are used to:}{Clean factory floors}{Forecast when industrial machines are likely to fail before breakdown occurs}{Design company logos}{Calculate employee salaries}

\mcqitem{36}{Why should users never enter confidential corporate passwords or sensitive student personal records into public AI tools?}{Because the AI will stop working}{To protect privacy and prevent sensitive data from potentially being stored or used for model retraining}{Because text inputs can only be 10 characters long}{Because AI cannot process names}

\mcqitem{37}{When an AI model generates unfair or discriminatory outputs favoring one social group over another, it is exhibiting:}{Hardware failure}{Algorithmic / Data Bias}{Overclocking}{High latency}

\mcqitem{38}{Why does algorithmic bias occur in AI systems?}{Because computers have personal feelings}{Because the model learns and replicates historical biases present in its human training data}{Because GPUs run too hot}{Because binary has only two states}

\mcqitem{39}{Representing an AI-generated essay as your own completely original academic work without disclosure is a violation of:}{Memory paging}{Academic integrity and ethics}{Software compilation}{Operating system licensing}

\mcqitem{40}{What is a "deepfake"?}{A very deep hard drive folder}{Realistic synthetic media (video or audio) manipulated or generated by AI to impersonate real individuals}{A slow internet connection}{An unindexed database}

\mcqitem{41}{Under responsible AI practice, what should be done when publishing synthetic images of simulated events?}{Claim they are real photographs}{Clearly label or disclose that the media is AI-generated}{Delete the original prompt}{Hide the creator's name}

\mcqitem{42}{Who holds ultimate ethical and legal accountability for decisions made using AI-assisted tools?}{The CPU manufacturer}{The human user or organization deploying the tool}{The monitor screen}{The programming language}

\mcqitem{43}{What should a student always do with factual statements and citations provided by a conversational LLM?}{Assume they are 100\% verified truth}{Independently verify them against authoritative reference sources}{Immediately delete them}{Ignore them}

\mcqitem{44}{Which of the following applications represents an AI use case in email services?}{Spam detection and smart reply autocomplete}{Cooling email servers}{Physical mail delivery}{Deleting the operating system}

\mcqitem{45}{Can modern generative AI models truly understand human feelings and possess independent consciousness?}{Yes, all LLMs are conscious}{No, they are statistical pattern-matching systems operating on numerical weights}{Only when running on laptops}{Only when written in Python}

\mcqitem{46}{Why is human oversight crucial in high-stakes AI applications like medical diagnosis and legal sentencing?}{Because AI systems are probabilistic and can make critical errors or lack contextual empathy}{Because humans want to type code}{Because computers are too fast}{Because AI cannot print documents}

\mcqitem{47}{Which component of an AI prompt specifies how the answer should be arranged (e.g., Markdown table, JSON, or numbered list)?}{Task}{Output format}{Role}{Token}

\mcqitem{48}{A prompt that states \texttt{"Summarize the 3 key causes of the Industrial Revolution in 150 words for high school students in a table"} has:}{No constraints}{Clear audience, topic, length, and format constraints}{Zero tokens}{Too many errors}

\mcqitem{49}{What is the relationship between AI, Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL)?}{They are three completely unrelated technologies}{AI is the broad field; ML is a subset of AI; DL is a specialized subset of ML}{DL contains ML, which contains AI}{They are different brands of computer monitors}

\begin{expbox}[Selected Question Explanations]
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm]
    \item \textbf{Q.1 (B):} The Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence in 1956 is widely recognized as the birthplace of AI as a formal academic discipline.
    \item \textbf{Q.7 (B):} All current commercial systems are Narrow (or Weak) AI because they are engineered to excel in specific, limited task domains.
    \item \textbf{Q.9 (B):} Hallucination occurs when an LLM predicts probable linguistic patterns that read plausibly but contradict actual facts or cite non-existent sources.
    \item \textbf{Q.14 (B):} Multimodal models like Google Gemini can process and generate mixtures of text, images, video, and audio simultaneously.
    \item \textbf{Q.23 (B):} NotebookLM strictly grounds its generation in the reference documents, notes, and PDFs uploaded by the user to reduce hallucinations.
    \item \textbf{Q.26 (B):} "Tell me about computers" has zero constraints, no defined task, no stated audience, and no formatting guidelines, making it a classic poor prompt.
    \item \textbf{Q.49 (B):} Artificial Intelligence is the overarching discipline; Machine Learning is a data-driven branch of AI; Deep Learning is a subset of ML using deep artificial neural networks.
\end{itemize}
\end{expbox}
