\noindent This textbook and laboratory companion has been engineered from the ground up to serve as the definitive, university-level practical guide for \textbf{ECE279: Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering Laboratory}. 

\section*{Course Architectural Philosophy}
Laboratory practice in electrical engineering and digital logic forms the physical bridge connecting abstract theoretical principles with real-world circuit implementation. Mastery of ECE279 requires an integrated understanding of:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Instrumentation \& Measurement Techniques:} Operating Digital Multimeters (DMM), Cathode Ray Oscilloscopes (CRO/DSO), Regulated DC Power Supplies (RPS), and Function Generators.
    \item \textbf{DC Circuit Analysis \& Conservation Laws:} Verifying Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) across multi-mesh resistive networks with rigorous sign convention tracking.
    \item \textbf{Semiconductor Junction Dynamics:} Characterizing $P$-$N$ junction diodes under forward and reverse bias, determining barrier knee potential, and calculating static ($R_{dc}$) and dynamic ($r_{ac}$) resistances.
    \item \textbf{Universal Digital Synthesis:} Constructing basic logic gates (NOT, AND, OR) exclusively from universal TTL gates (NAND IC 7400 and NOR IC 7402).
    \item \textbf{Combinational Arithmetic Circuits:} Synthesizing Half Adders and Full Adders using XOR, AND, and OR gates, deriving minterm expressions, and verifying 8-input truth tables.
    \item \textbf{Sequential Logic \& Bistable Storage:} Constructing level-triggered JK Flip-Flops using discrete 2-input and 3-input NAND gates, evaluating hold/set/reset/toggle states, and identifying Race-Around conditions.
\end{enumerate}
