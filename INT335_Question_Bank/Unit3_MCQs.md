# INT335 Question Bank

### Questions

**Q1. What is the primary objective of the Ideation stage in Design Thinking?**

B) Transitioning from problem definition to generating a broad range of potential solutions

B) Defer judgment and separate idea generation from idea evaluation

A) The physical flexibility of prototype materials
B) The number of different categories or domains represented among the ideas
