\chapter*{Preface}
This textbook presents a unified, rigorous, and modern pedagogical treatment of \textbf{PHY175: Modern Physics and Electronics}. Designed specifically for first-year engineering students, this text bridges the foundational physics of solid-state materials with practical digital systems, combinational and sequential circuit design, and hands-on microcontroller sensor interfacing.
