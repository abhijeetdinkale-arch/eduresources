### Course Modules & Syllabus Architecture
- **Part I / Unit 1**: Single-Variable Differential Calculus & Mean Value Theorems (Limits, Leibniz Rule, Rolle/LMVT/CMVT, Indeterminate Forms, Taylor/Maclaurin, Curvature, Asymptotes)
- **Part II / Unit 2**: Single-Variable Integral Calculus, Special Functions & Applications (Definite Integrals, Reduction Formulae, Walli's, Beta-Gamma Functions, Rectification, Quadrature, Volumes of Revolution)
- **Part III / Unit 3**: Infinite Sequences, Series & Convergence Tests (D'Alembert Ratio, Cauchy Root, Raabe's, Logarithmic, Integral, Alternating Series Leibniz Test, Power Series)
- **Part IV / Unit 4**: Multivariable Differential Calculus, Optimization & Jacobians (Limits, Partial Derivatives, Euler's Theorem, Total Differential, Extrema $rt-s^2$, Lagrange Multipliers, Jacobians)
- **Part V / Unit 5**: Multiple Integrals & Coordinate Transformations (Double Integrals, Change of Order, Polar Coordinates, Triple Integrals, Cylindrical/Spherical Polar, Mass/Centroids)
- **Part VI / Unit 6**: Vector Calculus & Field Integral Theorems (Gradient, Directional Derivative, Divergence, Curl, Solenoidal/Irrotational Fields, Line/Surface/Volume Integrals, Green's, Stokes', Gauss Divergence)
- **Part VII**: Master Formula, Method Recognition & Identity Sheet
- **Part VIII**: Multi-Unit Mixed Problem Practice Suites
- **Part IX**: Full-Length University Mock Examination Papers (100 Marks Each)
