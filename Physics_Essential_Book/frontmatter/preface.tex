\chapter*{Preface \& Pedagogical Architecture}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface \& Pedagogical Architecture}

Engineering Physics (\textbf{PHY110}) forms the bedrock of modern technical education. Every semiconductor transistor, fiber-optic backbone, medical laser, quantum encryption protocol, and high-frequency communication link relies directly on the foundational principles developed in this text.

This book has been curated to provide an intuitive yet mathematically rigorous exposition of the 6 core units of the PHY110 curriculum at Lovely Professional University.

\section*{Key Features of This Edition}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{First-Principles Mathematical Rigor:} Every critical law (Maxwell's equations, Einstein's $A$ and $B$ coefficients, the wave equation in conductors, 1D Schr\"odinger eigenfunctions, and the Clausius-Mossotti relation) is derived with step-by-step clarity.
    \item \textbf{Physical Intuition and Conceptual Clarity:} Rather than presenting isolated equations, the text explains the underlying physics, answering \textit{"Why did Maxwell introduce displacement current?"}, \textit{"Why must a laser have a metastable state?"}, and \textit{"Why does an electron have a finite zero-point energy in a quantum well?"}.
    \item \textbf{Engineering Case Studies \& Real-World Technologies:} Bridges theoretical physics to modern technologies such as 5G waveguide design, Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing (DWDM) optical networks, Scanning Tunneling Microscopy (STM), Hall effect sensors, and Superconducting Quantum Interference Devices (SQUIDs).
    \item \textbf{Worked Analytical Examples:} Illustrates practical numerical calculations across all units to bridge theory and examination mastery.
\end{enumerate}
