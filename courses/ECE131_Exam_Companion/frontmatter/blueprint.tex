\section*{Course Outcome Mapping}
The course \textbf{ECE131: Basic Electrical and Electronics Engineering} is structured around five core Course Outcomes (COs) mapped to ABET/NBA engineering graduate attributes:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{CO1 (D.C. Circuit Analysis)}: Apply fundamental circuit laws (Ohm's, KCL, KVL) and network reduction theorems (Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer) to analyze linear DC networks.
    \item \textbf{CO2 (A.C. Circuit Principles)}: Analyze single-phase and three-phase sinusoidal steady-state circuits, phasor relationships, resonance, and polyphase power measurement.
    \item \textbf{CO3 (Electrical Machines)}: Evaluate electromagnetic coupling principles, single-phase transformers, DC motor starting/speed control, and induction motor operating dynamics.
    \item \textbf{CO4 (Semiconductor Devices \& Amplifiers)}: Characterize semiconductor PN junctions, diodes, rectifiers, MOSFETs, and operational amplifier linear/non-linear circuits.
    \item \textbf{CO5 (Embedded Systems Interfacing)}: Design microcontroller-based data acquisition, ADC quantization, sensor signal conditioning, and IoT edge communication architectures.
\end{itemize}
