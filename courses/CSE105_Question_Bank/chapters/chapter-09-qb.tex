\section{Technical Viva-Voce Questions \& Conceptual Answers}

\subsection*{Q1. Explain the modal editing paradigm of vi and why it increases productivity.}
\begin{vivasol}
In modal editing, keys perform different operations depending on the current mode:
1. **Command Mode:** Keystrokes act as navigational vectors and text operators (\texttt{h,j,k,l}, \texttt{dd}, \texttt{yy}). Hands never leave the home row to reach for mouse or arrow keys.
2. **Insert Mode:** Keystrokes insert literal text.
3. **Ex Mode:** Accessed via colon (\texttt{:}) for file I/O, regex substitutions, and settings.
Separating text navigation/manipulation from text insertion drastically accelerates high-velocity programming.
\end{vivasol}
