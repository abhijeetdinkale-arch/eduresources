\subsection*{Examination Structure \& Weightage Overview}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Continuous Assessment 1 (CA-1):} 30 Marks, 60 Minutes. 6 Analytical Questions (5 Marks each). Covers CO1 and CO2.
    \item \textbf{Mid-Term Examination:} 30 Marks, 90 Minutes. Section A (10 MCQs, 10 Marks) + Section B (2 Analytical Problems, 10 Marks) + Section C (1 Case Study, 10 Marks). Covers CO1, CO2, CO3.
    \item \textbf{Continuous Assessment 2 (CA-2):} 30 Marks, 60 Minutes. 6 Applied Design Problems (5 Marks each). Covers CO4 and CO5.
    \item \textbf{End-Term Comprehensive Examination:} 50 Marks, 180 Minutes. Section A (10 MCQs, 10 Marks) + Section B (4 Scenarios, 20 Marks) + Section C (2 Capstone Challenges, 20 Marks). Covers CO1 through CO6.
\end{itemize}
