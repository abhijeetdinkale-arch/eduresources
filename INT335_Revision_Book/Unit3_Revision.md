# INT335: Design Thinking
## Unit 3: Ideation and Creative Problem Solving (Quick Revision Guide)

---

### 1. Key Concept Overview
* **Ideation**: The transition from understanding problems to generating candidate solutions.
* **Fundamental Rule**: **Separate idea generation (divergence) from idea evaluation (convergence)**.
* **Four Dimensions of Creative Ideas (Torrance Framework)**:
  1. **Fluency**: Total quantity of ideas produced.
  2. **Flexibility**: Diversity and range of categories covered.
  3. **Originality**: Uniqueness and novelty of concepts.
  4. **Elaboration**: Level of detail and development added.

---

### 2. Ideation Techniques & Tools
* **Brainstorming Rules (Alex Osborn)**:
  * Defer judgment / no early criticism.
  * Strive for volume (quantity breeds quality).
  * Encourage wild and unconventional ideas.
  * Build on the ideas of others (*"Yes, and..."*).
* **Brainwriting (6-3-5 Method)**: Silent generation on paper passed among teammates to eliminate dominant speaker bias.
* **SCAMPER Framework**:
  * **S** — Substitute (materials, components, steps)
  * **C** — Combine (integrate features or services)
  * **A** — Adapt (borrow solutions from other fields)
  * **M** — Modify / Magnify / Minify (change size, scale, speed)
  * **P** — Put to another use (repurpose existing assets)
  * **E** — Eliminate (remove complexity and redundant steps)
  * **R** — Reverse / Rearrange (invert sequences or user roles)
* **Mind Mapping**: Visual radial layout branching from a central challenge into associative themes.
* **Reverse Thinking (Inversion / Worst Possible Idea)**: Asking *"How could we make this problem worse?"* and inverting the answers to reveal hidden hazards and fresh solutions.

---

### 3. Organizing & Clustering Ideas
* **Affinity Diagramming**: Bottom-up clustering of ideas based on natural relationships and thematic patterns.
* **Concept Bundling**: Combining complementary small ideas into a cohesive, high-value solution package.

---

### 4. Feasibility, Screening & Prioritization
* **Multi-Criteria Evaluation**: Assessing ideas across Desirability, Technical Feasibility, and Business Viability.
* **Weighted Screening Matrix Formula**:
  $$T_i = \sum_{j=1}^n (w_j \times s_{ij})$$
  *(Where $w_j$ is criterion weight and $s_{ij}$ is the rating score)*.
* **Impact-Effort Matrix (2x2 Grid)**:
  * *High Impact, Low Effort*: **Quick Wins** (Implement first).
  * *High Impact, High Effort*: **Major Projects / Strategic Bets** (Plan carefully).
  * *Low Impact, Low Effort*: **Fill-ins / Minor Tweaks** (Do if time permits).
  * *Low Impact, High Effort*: **Thankless Tasks / Money Pits** (Drop/Avoid).
* **Voting Mechanisms**: Dot Voting (heat-mapping preferences) and token allocation.
